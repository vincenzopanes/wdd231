Welcome to Cobquecura

Cobquecura is a coastal town in the Ñuble Region of Chile. It is known for its Pacific beaches, unique rock formations, wildlife, historic architecture, and peaceful rural surroundings.

Whether you enjoy nature, history, photography, or outdoor activities, Cobquecura offers many places to explore.
